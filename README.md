# 💬 iMessage Mock Generator & Cinematic Video Exporter

A web-based studio tool for creators and video editors to craft authentic Apple iMessage conversations and export them as **1080p @ 60 FPS green-screen video animations** or **transparent PNG archives**.

Designed specifically for seamless compositing in **DaVinci Resolve**, **Adobe Premiere Pro**, **Final Cut Pro**, and **CapCut**.

---

### ✨ Key Features

- **🎬 60 FPS Video Recording**: Exports high-bitrate animated replays with zero drop-frames.
- **🎨 Chroma Key & Blend Options**:
  - **Chroma Green (`#00B140`)** & **Chroma Blue (`#0047BB`)** for clean Delta Keying.
  - **Pure Black (`#000000`)** for instant "Screen" or "Add" composite blend modes with zero color spill.
- **📐 Split-Screen Framing Presets**:
  - **Split-Screen Middle (740px)**: Keeps bubbles neatly centered between two actors in dialogue scenes.
  - **Standard (1060px)** & **Wide (1300px)**: For full-width landscape compositions.
- **↕️ Vertical Alignment**: Upper Third, Center, or Lower Third positioning.
- **🫧 Authentic iOS Spring Physics**: Realistic Apple overshoot and settle bubble pop-in.
- **🔔 Synchronized Audio FX**: Native Web Audio synthesizer baking iMessage swooshes and dings directly into the video audio track.
- **🌒 Cinematic Drop Shadow**: Soft ambient lighting under floating bubbles for legibility over complex video footage.
- **📷 Scene Still Matcher**: Overlay a video frame reference to preview positioning before hitting record.
- **📦 Transparent PNG Archive**: One-click ZIP export of individual transparent chat bubbles.
- **🌗 Default Dark Mode**: Sleek dark interface with responsive controls.
